Currently Head of R&D at Solafune · Geospatial ML and satellite remote sensing

GitHub LinkedIn Scholar

I lead research and development at Solafune, where we build satellite imagery applications for national governments and industry. I joined in 2023 as the company's first R&D member, built up the team, and led the prototypes and pitches that turned into partnerships with several national governments.

Before that I spent ten years as a planetary and atmospheric scientist, working on radiative transfer, remote sensing retrievals and large-scale climate simulation. I studied polarized light from exoplanet atmospheres at Caltech, the clouds of Venus with JAXA's Akatsuki orbiter at the University of Tokyo, and climate variability on exoplanets at the University of Bern.
